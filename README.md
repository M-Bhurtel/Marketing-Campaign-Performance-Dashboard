## 📌 Overview

This project presents a dynamic Excel dashboard built to analyze the performance of digital marketing campaigns across various advertising platforms. The goal was to uncover trends, optimize media spend, identify high-performing campaigns and products, and support data-driven decision-making for campaign strategy and budget allocation.

The dashboard provides actionable insights through a combination of KPIs, interactive visuals, filters, and campaign-level deep dives.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Interactive KPI Cards**  
  Displays Total Revenue, Total Conversions, Average ROAS, Top Campaign, and Lowest CPA with dynamic formatting and real-time updates.

- **Bubble Chart Analysis (ROAS vs CPA)**  
  Visualizes campaign-level trade-offs between efficiency (ROAS), cost (CPA), and scale (Conversions) using bubble size and axis placement.

- **Monthly Ad Spend Trend**  
  Line chart illustrating spend patterns across top-performing channels to support seasonal trend analysis.

- **Revenue by Product Category**  
  Donut chart visualizing product-level contribution to overall revenue, supporting product marketing decisions.

- **Insight Box (Executive Summary)**  
  Stakeholder-facing insight summary outlining top findings and next-step recommendations.

- **Slicers for Interactivity**  
  Filter the dashboard by Month, Channel, Campaign, Product Category, or Customer Segment to enable real-time scenario exploration.

## 💼 Business Impact

- Enabled strategic budget reallocation by identifying top-performing campaigns (e.g., Campaign 46: ROAS 15.00, CPA $2.11)
- Improved marketing ROI visibility across platforms and customer segments
